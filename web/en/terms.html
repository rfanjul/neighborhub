Last updated: 3 October 2026

By creating an account or using Neighborhub you accept these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the app.

1. What Neighborhub is

A platform for neighbors to ask for and offer help to each other. Neighborhub puts people in touch, but it doesn’t provide the services and isn’t a party to the arrangements you make with each other.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • Your account and profile details must be real, and you are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • One person, one account.

3. Prices, payments and fees

A request can be a free favor or have a price between CHF 5 and CHF 1,000, set by the person who asks. For paid requests:

  • The person who asks pays the price plus a service fee of 8% (at least CHF 1) when posting the request. Payments are processed by Stripe: Neighborhub never sees your card details.
  • Advertising, spam, soliciting customers for a business, or asking to be paid outside Neighborhub for a request posted here.
  • Until an offer is chosen, the person who asked can cancel the request and gets the full amount back, price and fee. Requests we don’t approve are refunded in full too. Once an offer is chosen the request can no longer be cancelled from the app; if something goes wrong, contact us and we’ll look into it.
  • To get paid, helpers set up payouts once with Stripe, which verifies their identity and bank account (IBAN). Payment services for helpers are provided by Stripe and are subject to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement, which includes the Stripe Terms of Service.
  • Helpers are responsible for declaring their income and for any taxes or social security contributions that apply.
  • If the person who asked doesn’t mark a completed request as done, we may do it after checking with both people.

Ratings and badges recognize help within the community: they have no monetary value and can’t be bought, sold or exchanged.

4. Community rules: zero tolerance

The following is not allowed in requests, profiles, photos, offers, chats or reviews:

  • Offensive, sexual, violent or discriminatory content, or content that incites hatred.
  • Harassment, threats, impersonating someone else or sharing other people’s personal data.
  • Illegal or dangerous services, or services that require a professional license (for example electrical or gas installations, or healthcare).
  • Asking for or offering money, advertising, spam or soliciting customers for a business.
  • Fake ratings or manipulating your own or someone else’s reputation.

5. Moderation and reports

We review requests before publishing them. You can report a request or a profile from the app or through the contact form. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules and may suspend or delete the accounts of those who break them, without prior notice in serious cases.

6. Your content

What you post remains yours. You allow us to show it within Neighborhub while it’s published. You must have the right to publish the photos and texts you upload.

7. Safety and liability

Meet in a visible place when possible, tell someone you trust when you’re going to give or receive help, and use common sense. The arrangements, the help and whatever happens during it are the responsibility of the people taking part. To the extent permitted by law, Neighborhub is not liable for damages arising from services arranged between users.

8. Leaving

You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → ⚙️ → Delete account). We may close accounts that break these terms.
