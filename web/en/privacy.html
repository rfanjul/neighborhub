Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Neighborhub is an app for neighbors to help each other. This policy explains what data we process, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the iPhone app and to this website. We comply with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Neighborhub, Zurich (Switzerland). You can write to us at any time using the contact form.

2. What data we process

  • Account: name, email and how you sign in (email and password, Apple or Google). If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only receive the relay address.
  • Profile: photo, bio, languages, city, postal code and, if you provide it, date of birth.
  • Requests you post: title, description, category, photos, duration, availability and the location where help is needed.
  • Activity: offers you make or receive, the chat messages of each request, ratings and reviews, your helps and badges.
  • Location: only while you use the app and with your permission, to calculate how far away requests are and to save the location of the ones you post. We don’t track your location in the background.
  • Camera and photos: only for the photos you choose to upload (profile and requests).
  • Contact: what you send us with the form (type of enquiry, name, email, message and language).
  • Preferences: the language you choose in the app, stored only on your iPhone.
  • Technical data: identifiers and logs generated by our infrastructure (Firebase) so the service works and stays secure.

3. What we use it for

  • Creating and maintaining your account and profile.
  • Showing nearby requests and handling offers, chat and ratings.
  • Reviewing requests before publishing them, handling reports and keeping the community safe.
  • Answering your questions and requests.

The legal basis is providing the service you ask for when you use the app, and our legitimate interest in keeping it safe. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

4. Who sees your data

  • Other neighbors with an account see your public profile: name, photo, bio, languages, city, ratings, reviews, helps and badges. Your email is not shown in the app.
  • Your approved requests are visible to neighbors with an account, with their location on the map.
  • The chat of a request can only be seen by the two people taking part in it.
  • Providers that help us run the service: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Hosting), Apple and Google for sign-in, and Apple Maps or Google Maps for the map. They may process data outside Switzerland or the EU, with the safeguards required by law (for example, standard contractual clauses).

5. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. If you delete it, we delete your account, your profile, your photos and your open requests and offers. Requests already completed with other neighbors and the reviews you left may be kept because they are part of their history; if you want us to delete them too, just ask. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for up to 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, take it with you, object to its processing or ask us to delete it. You can do almost everything from the app (Profile → ⚙️). For anything else, write to us using the contact form. If you believe we haven’t handled your data properly, you can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the authority in your country.

7. How to delete your account

From the app: Profile → ⚙️ → Delete account. If you can’t sign in to the app, ask us through the contact form with the email of your account.

8. Children

Neighborhub is not intended for anyone under 16 and we don’t knowingly collect data from children under that age.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited by security rules: for example, nobody can read a chat they’re not part of or change someone else’s ratings.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we’ll let you know in the app or on this website, with the date of the last update at the top.
